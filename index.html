Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NeighbourShare

How does the verification process work?

We verify every user through OTP verification and ID proof submission. This ensures a safe and trusted community for all users.

What if an item gets damaged?

We hold a security deposit during the rental period. If damage occurs, it's covered by the deposit. Optional insurance is available for high-value items.

How do I set the rental price?

You set your own price based on market rates. We provide suggested pricing based on similar items to help you stay competitive.

Is my payment secure?

Absolutely. We use secure payment gateways and hold payments in escrow until the item is returned in good condition.

What items can I list?

You can list any legal item that you own. Popular categories include tools, electronics, travel gear, party supplies, and kitchen appliances.

How far can I search for items?

Our platform focuses on hyper-local sharing within a 3km radius. This ensures quick pickups and builds stronger community connections.
